Company background

Who we are

Somali Resilience & Development Consultancy (SRDC) is a multidisciplinary consultancy firm headquartered in Baidoa, Southwest State of Somalia, with sub-offices in Mogadishu, Buurhakaba, Hudur, Qansaxdheere, Berdale and Jowhar.

We provide professional consultancy and technical services in humanitarian assistance, agriculture, food security, livelihoods, research, monitoring and evaluation, project management, institutional development and digital information management.
