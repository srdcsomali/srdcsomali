About Us

Company background, vision and mission

Somali Resilience & Development Consultancy (SRDC) is a multidisciplinary firm offering consultancy in humanitarian assistance, agriculture, food security, livelihoods, research, monitoring and evaluation, project management, institutional development and digital information management.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognized Somali consultancy firm delivering innovative, evidence-based and sustainable solutions for humanitarian response, agriculture and development.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality research, technical consultancy, monitoring and evaluation, and development solutions that strengthen organizations, inform decision-making and improve community resilience.
